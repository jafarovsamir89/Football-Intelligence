# Current limitations

- Football-Data.co.uk match-stat collection currently rotates only current-season division files and is bounded to at most two attempts per rolling seven days, including failed/404 requests. Coverage accumulates gradually and requires an exact same-date, same-orientation local fixture join. The app displays source-provided stats in recent result history only; they do not affect Elo predictions or backtests. It does not yet fetch prior-season stats or cover every competition.
- OpenLigaDB result parsing requires both its `matchIsFinished` flag and the semantic `After90Minutes` score for 1X2 settlement. Penalty/extra-time scores and unfinished rows remain unsettled; older payloads lacking semantic result kinds use the documented legacy result ID. The provider schema has no separate cancellation state, so missing results cannot safely be labeled cancelled. Existing rows are not rewritten by a migration; the parser correction applies to new/future sync data. Because the store preserves a previously complete score when a later response is incomplete, this change alone cannot retract a potentially premature score already saved by an older app version.

The app is an offline-first research notebook with experimental probabilities, not a validated betting product.

- Home now states the purpose and offers a direct button into the first featured match analysis. This improves navigation only: the model still has no validated bet/no-bet decision. Compose/API 26/API 34 checks passed in Actions [43438458641](https://github.com/jafarovsamir89/Football-Intelligence/actions/runs/43438458641); the card has not been observed on the physical phone.
- The active coupon workspace creates virtual outcome selections from experimental Elo marginals and, where available, fresh exact-linked bookmaker prices. It deliberately does not answer “should I bet?” or calculate conditional EV before enough independent prospective forecasts settle. The current Android UI warns about this; no future accuracy or profit is established. The latest coupon UI fix passed Actions [43435064071](https://github.com/jafarovsamir89/Football-Intelligence/actions/runs/43435064071) on API 34 and API 26, but has not been confirmed on the physical phone.

- The per-match Elo screen now exposes the numeric basis of a forecast: pre-kickoff team ratings, home-advantage points, eligible league/team result counts, and the smoothed competition draw-rate reference. The highest marginal probability describes only the model's most likely 1X2 outcome; it is not a bet instruction or a validated joint coupon chance. Live Elo remains uncalibrated and does not model lineups, injuries, or other match-day information. Prospective snapshots are saved for later outcome checks, but no independent prospective edge has been established.

- The selected-match view can highlight Elo's largest positive difference from a fresh Misli/TOPAZ 1X2 market after proportional margin removal. The common test threshold only avoids floating-point noise; it is not a statistically justified minimum edge. The comparison is descriptive, the model is uncalibrated, and neither the comparison nor a virtual coupon is a recommendation or evidence of positive expected value.

- Selected-match analysis can summarize the first and latest locally stored pre-kickoff Misli/TOPAZ 1X2 observations for one exact-linked fixture and show their capture times and raw-price percentage change. The snapshots accumulate only when foreground WebView capture succeeds. They are not operator-designated opening/closing prices, CLV, a continuous history feed, or evidence of value; duplicate captures with conflicting values and histories spanning multiple app-generated event keys (derived from visible date, time and team labels) are not summarized.

- Price-backed virtual coupons may show the product of their own fresh bookmaker prices, but the app does not calculate expected return for future coupons until the current model has an independent prospective validation. A price is not an edge. Retrospective settled-coupon ROI, when fully price-covered, describes only that recorded sample and does not predict future return.

- OpenFootball and OpenLigaDB sync are implemented with local persistence, provider timestamps, retry/backoff, cache and duplicate suppression. A disposable API 34 first-run completed across five OpenFootball seasons and five OpenLigaDB year catalogs: 38,845 merged matches, 29,612 completed results, 184 competition labels and 125 future forecast snapshots. An offline check left the local database readable. No physical-device football data-sync run is recorded yet.
- OpenLigaDB is community-maintained. The app discovers football competitions for five calendar-year catalogs. Each Worker reserves up to three minutes for OpenFootball and allows OpenLigaDB to continue until the seven-minute total source deadline, then queues remaining history. WorkManager is best-effort and Android may defer work.
- The Home sync indicator describes the newest persisted provider attempt, not an always-online connection. A failed source is shown as an error even when an older successful timestamp exists; partial/deferred work is neutral and saved database content remains available offline. Android can still delay queued jobs, so the indicator cannot promise an exact refresh interval.
- OpenFootball rows without exact kickoff time are included in the upcoming Home/Matches list by local date and labeled as lacking a start time; they still cannot generate a kickoff-based forecast or exact local bookmaker quote. Unit/UI tests, APK builds, and API 26/34 instrumentation passed (run [43419141410](https://github.com/jafarovsamir89/Football-Intelligence/actions/runs/43419141410)); no physical-phone check was possible. International odds may link such a row only when exact normalized home/away order and UTC date identify exactly one unfinished fixture; the UI records that the link is date-only, not exact kickoff. Multiple same-day candidates stay unmatched. Historical results are held until the conservative availability date.
- Cross-feed deduplication is intentionally strict: same event date, normalized team names, and same home/away order. If names do not normalize exactly or multiple same-day candidate fixtures exist, both records remain rather than guessing.

Older 9/25-line captures have expired. No hidden endpoint or evasion fallback is used. Explicit automated odds-reuse permission remains unconfirmed; snapshots stay on-device. - TipsAudit is integrated only as a *derived market reference*, not as a bookmaker. The live POC returned 98 records at 2026-11-11 23:22 UTC, but its Türkiye—Belgium fair read was ~47 minutes old and therefore rejected by the app's 30-minute rule. The current capture supplied no eligible comparison row. The app ignores that service's soft prices/value flags and uses no TipsAudit value as an operator payout, EV input or coupon price. Any comparison is single-feed descriptive probability difference, not independent consensus. Pinnacle API access is inquiry/variable-cost, Betfair live API requires credentials and a £499 activation fee, and bet365 terms restrict automated extraction; those providers have no verified free/no-key adapter.
- The app builds same-bookmaker price-backed virtual accumulators when every leg has a fresh exact fixture match. At the historical capture Misli had no exact local fixture links, so no Misli price-backed coupon was created; TOPAZ generated separate candidates from actual source prices, which expire from fresh use after 30 minutes. Model-only drafts remain available without prices. Joint probability uses Fréchet lower/upper bounds; no independence model or real payout claim exists. A coupon is marked lost after any incorrect finished leg, even if others are pending. ROI, profit factor and drawdown are withheld whenever any settled coupon in the evaluated group has no full combined price; the UI reports price coverage separately. No prospective profitability has been established.
- A separate local market-disagreement draft compares Elo marginals with de-vigged probabilities from the same exact-linked, fresh complete Misli or TOPAZ 1X2 line. It stores each selected fair probability and difference. This is descriptive only: Elo is uncalibrated, no EV/ROI/profit metric is displayed for this basis, and these selections do not prove an edge.
- The local DB includes 566 legacy placeholder-team rows. They are retained for migration safety but hidden from normal results and excluded from forecasts, picks, fixture linking, accumulators and walk-forward; parser/upsert paths reject new placeholders. Post-filter automatic-source walk-forward: 29,588 targets, 19,185 forecasts, 10,403 insufficient-history skips, Brier 0.6106, Log Loss 1.0235. This is still retrospective and uncalibrated.
- Automatic coupon drafts select the top available Elo marginal per eligible fixture. This is not a value filter or validated recommendation. No probability calibration, independent forward test, stake sizing, or dependence/correlation model exists. Accumulator outcomes are checked against saved local results; a kickoff move above 30 minutes is marked for review instead of treated as a loss.
- The walk-forward screen reports retrospective one-vs-rest ECE and reliability bins by outcome. The app does not apply a calibration transform, and the values rely on current source snapshots rather than archived vintages or prospectively frozen forecasts.
- Automatic Elo walk-forward scores are also grouped by the exact competition and season labels stored with source rows; missing labels remain “unknown” and are never inferred from kickoff dates. OpenFootball and OpenLigaDB may use different season conventions (multi-year season versus calendar year), so labels are not normalized across providers. Small groups are shown for transparency but marked n<30 and are not enough to support a league/season quality conclusion.
- The v11 prospective journal is CI- and API 34 runtime-verified. The latest disposable first-run held 125 future snapshots and 0 settled results; no prospective accuracy estimate is available. The Odds API quote capture preceded the saved forecast baseline, so it supplied no initial paired market/Elo comparison. A scheduler gate now waits for football sync and a saved forecast baseline; this passed Actions [43389646589](https://github.com/jafarovsamir89/Football-Intelligence/actions/runs/43389646589), but the physical phone still needs a data-preserving update and reachability check. The evaluator reads the full local ledger, selects one latest pre-kickoff forecast per fixture/model version, and validates the stored score, result source and post-kickoff availability time. From 30 settled fixtures it computes paired-bootstrap intervals; no prospective superiority result has been established.
- Full-screen prospective and market diagnostics are computed asynchronously on a CPU dispatcher. While a new snapshot is being evaluated, the summary cards show an updating state and hide prior aggregate metrics; current source rows and saved history remain visible. The scoring input remains the full forecast ledger even though recent history shows at most 100 rows.
- Elo uses production defaults and requires at least 10 prior same-league matches and 3 appearances per team. A separate 27-setting K/home-advantage/draw-prior grid is selected on a chronological validation segment and then evaluated on a final holdout; it requires at least 100 validation forecasts and does not change live settings. The Poisson baseline estimates goal rates from goals scored/conceded, not external expected-goals data; it requires 50 league matches and 5 team appearances. Dixon–Coles rho is selected from a five-value grid on validation Log Loss only when at least 100 validation forecasts exist; otherwise rho stays at zero. The paired holdout excludes fixtures where either model lacks a forecast. Validation is a tuning set, while only the final holdout is used for the comparison; both are one retrospective split of current files rather than archived vintages and may reflect snapshot-selection bias. Probabilities remain uncalibrated and these models do not establish advantage.
- The venue-split Home/Away baseline is a separate, uncalibrated research comparison. It uses only home-team home results and away-team away results with venue-specific shrinkage and coverage thresholds. It is not used to produce live forecasts or coupons. Its validation and holdout metrics inherit the same current-file vintage limitations as the other retrospective models; no measured advantage is established.
- The Elo/Poisson combined model remains a research experiment. It searches a fixed 9×9 grid of Elo weights and shared multiclass temperature scaling, fitting Log Loss only on a shared chronological validation sample. It requires at least 500 common forecasts and improvement in both Brier and Log Loss against both simple models on validation and untouched holdout. One-vs-rest ECE is reported for all three methods on both splits. This is one split of current files without archived vintages; it does not replace live Elo or drive coupons, and cannot establish future performance by itself.
- Paired Elo-minus-Poisson and Elo-minus-Home/Away 95% bootstrap intervals are shown only from 30 shared holdout fixtures, using 2,000 deterministic resamples. They assume independent fixtures; recurring teams/rounds can understate uncertainty. The intervals are descriptive and do not validate the model for future bets.
- CSV walk-forward remains optional in Settings → Additional. The app also runs a walk-forward over automatic-source results; its source-availability assumptions and lack of archived versions limit historical interpretation.
- The app does not import crowd/tipster picks. No free permitted automated tipster feed was confirmed.
- The previous manual quote/ticket database is preserved for upgrades, but manual quote entry is not part of the current main user flow. No real bet or bookmaker account action exists.

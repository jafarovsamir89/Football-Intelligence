## Odds and market data

### Azerbaijan operator check (2026-11-09)

- A standard Android API 34 WebView rendered Misli's public football page and visible future date, kickoff, team labels and complete 1/X/2 lines. The latest foreground capture held 9 future rows (capture time 2026-11-10 04:21:34 UTC); 0 linked to an exact local forecast fixture. Five initial Women's Champions League fixtures had matched the [official UEFA schedule](https://www.uefa.com/womenschampionsleague/news/02a9-21848dfb62c3-1313bc23426c-1000--2026-27-womens-champions-league-all-the-league-phase-fixtu/), but they were not available in the local fixture set for Elo comparison.
- The collector reads only visible `document.body.innerText` from `https://www.misli.az/idman-novleri/futbol`. Its [robots.txt](https://www.misli.az/robots.txt) allows the public route, but does not grant a data-reuse license; no explicit automated-reuse permission was found. Snapshots stay on-device, are hidden after 30 minutes, and the WebView is loaded automatically on app open. WebView refresh is foreground-only; WorkManager does not run this page collector in the background.
- On the latest ordinary WebView capture, eTopaz's visible `İdman` tab returned 25 complete future 1/X/2 lines at 2026-11-10 04:21:37 UTC. Seven quotes linked to local fixtures with unique exact alias/orientation/date/kickoff matches. Portugal—Denmark match 85257 again had H/D/A 1.45/4.31/5.29; the coupon screen compared those saved prices with a 59.1% Elo home marginal. The earlier Türkiye–Belgium 3.30/3.64/1.88 quote links to match 85220 and was separately cross-checked against the [UEFA Nations League schedule](https://www.uefa.com/uefanationsleague/news/02a2-1fea7079900a-801b2c5f6c9e-1000--2026-uefa-nations-league-league-phase-fixtures-and-resul/). The page omits year; the app infers the next occurrence only within 90 days and records that provenance.
- The [official eTopaz terms](https://etopaz.az/terms-conditions) did not establish permission for automated reuse of anonymous public odds; `robots.txt` was not retrievable in this check. Misli robots rules allow its public route but do not grant a reuse license. Automated reuse permission remains unconfirmed for both operators. Snapshots stay local and are not redistributed; technical capture does not establish legal authorization.
- Reviewed [aliabbasov99/ai-football-prediction](https://github.com/aliabbasov99/ai-football-prediction) and its [Misli scraper](https://github.com/aliabbasov99/ai-football-prediction/blob/main/backend/scripts/scrape_misli_all.py) for explicit alias-table ideas. Its stealth/evasion techniques and fuzzy matching were not copied. This app uses a small reviewed alias list and exact home/away/date/kickoff matching only.

Both technical integrations were observed on one API 34 emulator for real public-page snapshots and exact TOPAZ fixture joins; this does not resolve reuse rights, guarantee that a line remains open, or establish expected profit. Quote freshness is 30 minutes. The 04:21 UTC capture produced a two-leg TOPAZ price-backed virtual draft using the saved England—Croatia price 1.21 and Portugal—Denmark price 1.45; joint probability is shown as bounds only. Older 2/3/5-leg draft observations are historical snapshots and are not current prices. No permitted, dependable free automated crowd/tipster feed was confirmed. Neither operator source is replaced by third-party "fair odds."

## Decision

The app uses OpenLigaDB and OpenFootball for fixtures/results and separate Misli/eTopaz foreground WebView collectors for local short-lived odds snapshots. Manual odds/ticket records remain preserved for older databases but are not part of the normal UI. A user-selected Football-Data-style CSV is optional in Settings. No automated crowd/tipster source or bookmaker odds history archive has been verified. We do not access private or restricted services to fill those gaps.

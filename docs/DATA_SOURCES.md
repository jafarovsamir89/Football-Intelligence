Portugal—Denmark was 1.47/4.24/5.14 and linked to local fixture 85257. These time-bound quotes expire for fresh display after 30 minutes; they are evidence of technical capture, not a current price after that window.
- The gated collector from `fd51aa4` automatically reopened both pages after that 30-minute window while the app stayed foreground. At 21:34:53 UTC (Misli) and 21:35:11 UTC (eTopaz), neither page had verified future prematch lines. Both providers were marked `UNAVAILABLE`; no rows were fabricated, and the stored 128/400 snapshot history remained unchanged. The [eTopaz robots.txt](https://etopaz.az/robots.txt) could not be fetched in the web review; this is not evidence of permission or prohibition. Explicit automated odds-reuse permission remains unconfirmed for both operators.
- Reviewed [aliabbasov99/ai-football-prediction](https://github.com/aliabbasov99/ai-football-prediction) and its [Misli scraper](https://github.com/aliabbasov99/ai-football-prediction/blob/main/backend/scripts/scrape_misli_all.py). Both integrations have produced time-stamped public-page snapshots on API 34; TOPAZ exact fixture joins were observed, while the most recent Misli capture had no exact local joins. #### Direct POC evidence

An unauthenticated `GET https://tipsaudit.com/api/v1/predictions` on 2026-11-11 23:22:04 UTC returned 98 future records with methodology version `2.0`. Türkiye—Belgium (`id=16388`, 2026-11-12 17:00 UTC) matched local fixture `85220` by exact reviewed team identity and kickoff. Its record said `fair_source=live`, but `fair_read_at=2026-11-11 22:34:39 UTC`, about 47 minutes before API capture: it fails this app's 30-minute freshness filter and contributes no current value comparison. The endpoint was technically reachable, but this capture proved no fresh usable quote. Current future availability can change; do not claim a live line from this historical POC.

The Android implementation uses a separate SQLite v12 market-reference ledger and an independent network-constrained Worker (15-minute app-open staleness check; 30-minute periodic best-effort schedule). It requires pre-match timestamps, bounded source freshness, coherent 1X2 probabilities and reciprocal fair prices, an allowed HTTPS host, and exact unique fixture matching. The raw operator-odds table and virtual coupon builder do not consume this feed. On an unavailable/stale response the UI says so and uses the offline local database.

Pinnacle's [official API page](https://www.pinnacle.com/en/api) lists inquiry-based access and variable pricing. Betfair's [official getting-started guide](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003864651-How-do-I-get-started) specifies account/key/session requirements and a £499 live-key fee. bet365's [official terms](https://help.bet365.com/s/en/terms-and-conditions) restrict automated extraction. These constraints leave no verified free, no-account raw feed for those three named providers. Do not infer that a third-party fair estimate is a direct provider adapter.

#### MoviOdds public proof-of-concept

At `2026-11-12T00:39:24Z`, one ordinary unauthenticated HTTPS GET returned 923 soccer match rows and 2,712 group-40 quotes. Applying the app's prematch, future kickoff, full 1/X/2, unsuspended-market, source-poll (≤15 minutes), timestamp and duplicate checks retained 904 rows. Club America (W)—Toluca (W), Liga MX Femenil, 12 Nov 2026 01:00 UTC, returned 1.20/7.00/8.50. Its individual quote `ts` values were 00:23:59 and 00:09:08 UTC; group-40 `polled_at` was 00:39:24 UTC. This verifies why last-price-change time and successful market-read time must be stored separately. These are historical observations after the capture time, not current prices. The feed is not a first-party bet365 API, and MoviOdds states it is independent and unaffiliated. The in-app worker is paused until the user confirms age 18+ as required by MoviOdds terms; football-result sync is unaffected.

The Android adapter preserves source `ts` per outcome, provider `polled_at`, and app capture time independently. It accepts only group 40 with exactly one home/draw/away selection, a future prematch fixture, valid decimal prices, no suspension, a recent non-stale market read and a verified device/source clock. Missing poll metadata is `UNVERIFIED`; stale lines are excluded. HTTP access refusal becomes `BLOCKED`, transient/request/parser failures become `ERROR`, and no verified future line becomes `UNAVAILABLE`.

SQLite v13 uses a current-market table refreshed at each successful poll and a 30-day price-change history that appends only when a decimal price or its source `ts` changes. This avoids storing hundreds of thousands of identical rows while preserving offline history and line movement. Exact normalized home/away orientation, UTC date, one unique local fixture and kickoff within 20 minutes are required for local comparisons. No fuzzy matching is used. The market engine removes margin proportionally and deduplicates one underlying bookmaker/event reported by multiple intermediaries. With one underlying bookmaker, the UI calls the result a “one-book reference” and does not claim market consensus.

International prices are shown separately from Misli and TOPAZ, never enter local virtual coupons, and produce no EV while the model lacks prospective calibration. Public JSON access from Azerbaijan on a physical handset has not been tested; regional availability remains unverified. WorkManager can defer requests; cached quotes and price changes remain available offline with historical labeling.

## Decision

The app uses OpenLigaDB/OpenFootball for fixtures/results, separate Misli/eTopaz foreground WebView collectors for local short-lived odds, TipsAudit for an attributed derived fair-market reference, and MoviOdds' documented free endpoint for bet365 odds. No Oddsbunch, BetExplorer, Oddspedia, OddsPortal, Oddschecker, or tipster content is automatically ingested because applicable reuse permission is absent or account/region restricted. Manual odds/ticket records remain preserved for old databases but are not in the normal user flow. A user-selected CSV remains optional in Settings. International quotes are locally timestamped and isolated from Misli/TOPAZ coupon pricing.

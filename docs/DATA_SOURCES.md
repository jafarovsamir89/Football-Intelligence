### Automatic match statistics (personal-use source)

Football-Data.co.uk's [usage policy](https://football-data.co.uk/contact.php) allows private personal betting/academic research using manual downloads or at most light automation (twice weekly maximum) and excludes commercial/data-training projects involving heavy repeated automated bots/scrapers/AI. The Android adapter follows a stricter, persisted gate: it reserves an attempt before opening a connection, allows at most two attempts in any rolling seven-day period, and rotates one current-season league CSV per allowed request. Failed and unavailable HTTP attempts also consume the local allowance; this avoids retry bursts.

The worker recognizes only the match-stat headers `HS/AS`, `HST/AST`, `HC/AC`, `HF/AF`, `HY/AY`, and `HR/AR`. It does not transform odds fields into prices or store the source CSV body. Stats attach only to a single local completed match with the same date and exact canonical home/away orientation. Each record keeps its source URL, capture time, and an availability bound no earlier than capture or two UTC days after the listed event date. The screen displays these fields on the Results tab; they do not enter Elo, coupon selection, model training, or backtests. Coverage is progressive (one of the listed European divisions per request) and limited to current-season files; historical stats seasons are not yet downloaded. The source does not cover the app's full set of competitions, including Azerbaijan.

#### MoviOdds public proof-of-concept

At `2026-11-12T00:39:24Z`, one ordinary unauthenticated HTTPS GET returned 923 soccer match rows and 2,712 group-40 quotes. Applying the app's prematch, future kickoff, full 1/X/2, unsuspended-market, source-poll (≤15 minutes), timestamp and duplicate checks retained 904 rows. For a historical match, individual quote-change timestamps differed from the group-40 poll timestamp. This verifies why last-price-change time and successful market-read time must be stored separately. These are historical observations, not current prices. The feed is not a first-party bet365 API, and MoviOdds states it is independent and unaffiliated. The in-app worker is paused until the user confirms age 18+ as required by MoviOdds terms; football-result sync is unaffected.

The Android adapter preserves source `ts` per outcome, provider `polled_at`, and app capture time independently. It accepts only group 40 with exactly one home/draw/away selection, a future prematch fixture, valid decimal prices, no suspension, a recent non-stale market read and a verified device/source clock. The latest parse's received/accepted/rejected/stale/duplicate/unverified row counts are retained locally and shown in the World Market section; they describe one response and are not an estimated reliability score. Missing poll metadata is `UNVERIFIED`; stale lines are excluded. HTTP access refusal becomes `BLOCKED`, transient/request/parser failures become `ERROR`, and no verified future line becomes `UNAVAILABLE`.

SQLite v18 retains the current-market table introduced in v13, refreshed at each successful poll, and a 30-day price-change history that appends only when a decimal price, source timestamp, or source bet limit changes. It also stores the latest parsed-response quality counters, a 30-day sync-attempt history with status/available parse counts, and each local link's exact-kickoff vs unique-date-only basis. The v17→v18 migration preserves prior rows, classifies them as Back, and retains IDs and fixture-link precision. The new source-market and quote-side dimensions let Back and Lay coexist for the same event and capture. This is observed history, not a calculated uptime/reliability score. The separated quote history avoids storing hundreds of thousands of identical rows while preserving offline inspection and line movement. Local comparisons require exact normalized home/away orientation and UTC date; an exact local kickoff must be within 20 minutes, while a date-only local row must be the only unfinished same-day team-pair candidate. No fuzzy matching is used. The market engine removes margin proportionally from Back quotes only and deduplicates one underlying bookmaker/event reported by multiple intermediaries. Lay is not an independent book or a prospective market/Elo evaluation input. A Betfair spread is shown only for the same source capture. No commission is supplied or deducted, and bet limits are not treated as liquidity. With one underlying bookmaker, the UI calls the result a “one-book reference” and does not claim market consensus.

International prices are shown separately from Misli and TOPAZ, never enter local virtual coupons, and produce no EV while the model lacks prospective calibration. Public JSON access from Azerbaijan on a physical handset has not been tested; regional availability remains unverified. WorkManager can defer requests; cached quotes and price changes remain available offline with historical labeling.

The World Market's paired baseline uses only its locally retained 30-day/5,000-row quote-change history. It keeps one latest fresh pre-kickoff line per linked fixture and underlying bookmaker, pairs it only with a saved Elo forecast already present by the app capture, and scores both after a timestamped post-kickoff result becomes available. The report is bounded by device-local retention and does not represent closing odds or CLV.

## Decision

The app uses OpenLigaDB/OpenFootball for fixtures/results, separate Misli/eTopaz foreground WebView collectors for local short-lived odds, TipsAudit for an attributed derived fair-market reference, and MoviOdds' documented free endpoint for bet365 odds. No Oddsbunch, BetExplorer, Oddspedia, OddsPortal, Oddschecker, or tipster content is automatically ingested because applicable reuse permission is absent or account/region restricted. Manual odds/ticket records remain preserved for old databases but are not in the normal user flow. A user-selected CSV remains optional in Settings. International quotes are locally timestamped and isolated from Misli/TOPAZ coupon pricing.
